Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Effective immediately

1. Introduction

TutorDesk ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using TutorDesk, you agree to this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for authentication and account recovery)
  • Name (displayed within your workspace)
  • Account type (Solo or Business)
  • Business name (if Business account)

Workspace Data (Provided by You)

  • Student and parent contact details
  • Session schedules, attendance records, and notes
  • Invoice and payment records
  • Gradebook entries and progress tracking data
  • Uploaded resources (documents, PDFs, images)

Automatic Data

  • Authentication logs (login timestamps, IP address for security)
  • Usage analytics (feature interaction, error reporting)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and maintain the TutorDesk service
  • Authenticate users and enforce workspace isolation
  • Enable Student and Parent portal access
  • Send transactional emails (invoices, portal codes, password resets)
  • Improve performance and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) — EU/US regions based on project config
  • Encryption: All traffic encrypted via TLS 1.2+; data encrypted at rest
  • Isolation: Row-level security enforces workspace (`org_id`) separation — no cross-workspace access
  • Authentication: Supabase Auth (OAuth providers + email/password with secure hashing)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. Data is only shared:

  • Within your workspace (team members you invite)
  • With students/parents you grant portal access to
  • With subprocessors (Supabase for hosting/auth, email delivery services) under DPAs
  • When required by law or to protect rights/safety

6. Your Rights

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of your data from Profile → Export Data
  • Correction: Edit profile and workspace data anytime in-app
  • Deletion: Delete your account in Profile → Danger Zone (removes all workspace data)
  • Objection/Restriction: Contact us to limit processing

7. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: retained while account exists
  • Deleted accounts: purged within 30 days (backups up to 90 days)
  • Auth logs: retained 12 months for security

8. Children's Privacy

TutorDesk is not directed to children under 13. Student portal accounts are created by tutors/parents. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent. If you believe we have such data, contact us for immediate deletion.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own (Supabase regions). Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in-app and via email 30 days before taking effect. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
